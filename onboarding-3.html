Screen 3 — Readiness checklist · design notes

  • Directly attacks the 62% KYC drop-off and the #1 support complaint ("didn't know I needed PAN and Aadhaar ready"). The failure happened before KYC — so the fix lives before KYC too.
  • The CTA stays disabled until both boxes are ticked. A tiny commitment device: users who prepare don't abandon mid-flow.
  • Beej's microcopy normalises going slow ("I'll wait") — the opposite of a pressure funnel, which is exactly what a scared first-timer needs.
  • Checkboxes are 26px with a full-row 44px+ tap area; labels wrap generously for Hindi-length strings.
