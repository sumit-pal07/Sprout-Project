Screen 4 — Honest KYC · design notes

  • Directly answers complaint #2: "the app asked me to wait and I thought it crashed." Every wait now has a named step, a time expectation ("about 20 seconds"), and a normaliser ("nothing is stuck").
  • Progress is truthful, not decorative. Steps flip to ✓ only when done; the active step names what it's waiting on. No fake progress bars.
  • The prototype auto-advances to demo the flow; production would poll real verification APIs with the same copy.
  • Beej returns at the success moment — the seedling "grows" (bigger smile) as the user progresses. Small emotional payoff, zero extra weight.
