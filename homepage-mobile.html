Mobile homepage — design notes

  • One thumb, one action. A single primary CTA (52px tall) — no competing banners or pop-ups fighting a 78% bounce rate.
  • Disclaimer is structural, not a footnote. A sticky 12px bar, always visible — compliance without scrolling.
  • Language hint up top (EN | हिं) signals Hindi is coming; layout already leaves room for longer Devanagari strings.
  • Beej appears at two sizes from the same SVG — brand continuity into onboarding at ~2KB cost.
  • No video, no ticker autoplay, no pop-ups: keeps the page far under the 1.5MB budget on 4G.
