Screen 5 — First investment · design notes

  • ₹100 is the default — the smallest commitment that still feels real. Larger options exist but aren't pushed.
  • Disclaimer sits above the CTA, ≥12px, no scroll needed. Compliance is part of the layout, not a link to it.
  • Referral appears only after success — asking before the first investment would tax trust at its most fragile moment. Post-success, it's a celebration mechanic.
  • Beej's leaves are drawn slightly larger here — the seedling "grows" with the user's progress. One SVG, endless story.
  • Copy check: none of the prohibited promise-words appear anywhere in user-facing copy.
