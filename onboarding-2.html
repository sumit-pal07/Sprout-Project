Screen 2 — How it works · design notes

  • Previews the entire journey up front. First-timers drop off when step 3 surprises them; showing all three steps now sets honest expectations.
  • Beej shrinks to a guide badge — same character, smaller role. Continuity without clutter.
  • The CTA copy ("what's needed?") tees up screen 3's checklist — the story flows, not jumps.
  • No returns projections and none of the prohibited promise-words appear anywhere in user-facing copy — compliance-safe by construction.
